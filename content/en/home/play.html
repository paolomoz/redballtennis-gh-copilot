HOW TO PLAY

  • Players decide who serves first
  • Serve underhand, cross-court, with two chances to put the ball in play
  • The ball must bounce once on the return; no volleys!
  • Switch sides after every odd game

HOW TO SCORE

  • First to 4 points wins the game
  • First to 4 games wins the set
  • The first player/team to win 2 out of 3 sets wins the match
  • Or play a timed match and the team with the most games wins

WHAT YOU NEED TO PLAY

23-INCH RACQUETS

(smaller than a traditional tennis racquet)

TENNIS "RED BALL"

(lower compression tennis ball with slower bounce)

WHERE TO PLAY

Your local tennis court, a pickleball court, a brewery, a parking lot, your driveway—the opportunities to play Red Ball are endless. Just pop up a net on any hard surface and get playing!
